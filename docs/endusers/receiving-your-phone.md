# Receive and accept your phone

Complete this checklist with the operator before relying on the phone. Use the
accepted report and dated notes for your specific device; the general booklet
describes intended configuration.

## Match the device and packet

Check the asset label on the physical phone against the report and inserts.
Privately compare hardware serial details where supplied. Confirm which person
will use the phone, which language they need, and which profile serves each purpose.
If you are also the operator, check these items yourself before recording custody.

Your packet should include:

- A quick start matching the intended policy and your chosen English/Polish language.
- Your phone’s accepted handoff report and dated notes listing actual configuration,

- Privately completed ordinary credential/recovery material and the real support route.

Missing values in an exported insert mean not recorded, which does not establish
whether the feature or account is configured. Ask the operator to resolve ambiguity

## Check ordinary access privately

Open every promised profile, confirm its language and apps, and perform an ordinary
lock/unlock with that profile’s correct PIN or password. Privately verify or replace
initial defaults as agreed. Do not read secrets aloud or put them into support notes.

Complete private app setup, imports, MFA, and recovery access in the intended
profile. Check required VPN connections and actual app behavior with the operator.
Raise missing apps, broken connectivity, incorrect profile identities, or unexplained
warnings. Review [credential distinctions](pins-passwords-and-duress.md) before
any duress setup; do not test destructive activation.

## Confirm the security state

With the operator, physically check that USB debugging and Wireless debugging are
both off after finalization. Confirm the agreed locked-bootloader/OEM-unlocking
state. Learn how to switch profiles, lock them, and end a secondary-user session
on this exact build.

Review backup coverage and exclusions for every profile and important app. Ask how
you regain access when this phone, its number, or its MFA is unavailable. A backup
notification is not a demonstrated restore; agree what was actually checked.

## Complete the handoff

The operator records your actual acceptance checks before final signoff and later
records transfer of custody after you have the phone and debugging is off. If
required checks are missing or contradicted by what you see, resolve them before
the phone is described as ready.

separate from ordinary unlock material, and protect spare prints and digital copies.
Know the actual support contact and the [loss and recovery procedure](backup-loss-and-support.md).
