# Protect your security and privacy

Use the phone according to its agreed profile and app purposes. Your handoff notes
describe the checks and limitations for this exact installation; a privacy label,
installed app, or passing helper test is not a guarantee about every activity.

## Keep activities in their intended profiles

Android profiles separate app data and many settings. Enter an account or import
private material only after checking the active profile. Installing the same app
in another profile does not copy its sign-in or account setup automatically.

App package versions are shared across the phone. Updating or removing a package
can affect other profiles. A user number or profile name can be reused after
deletion; recreating a profile with the same name does not restore its previous
identity or data. Ask the operator before deleting a profile or replacing managed apps.

Learn the difference between switching away, locking, and ending a secondary
session on your exact build. Follow the agreed method for the activity rather than
assuming a profile switch ends all work in the previous profile.

## Enter secrets privately

Use [ordinary unlock credentials](pins-passwords-and-duress.md) for authentication.
Complete account passwords, MFA, sensitive imports, and recovery setup privately.
Keep recovery material accessible without depending only on this phone or its number.

Review app permissions and Android consent before accepting them. If a requested
permission, screen, or import differs from the reviewed instructions, stop and ask.
Do not paste credentials or sensitive configuration into general notes, screenshots,
AI chats, shared guide files, or troubleshooting logs.

## Understand the network checks

Configure the approved VPN app and Android consent separately in every profile
where required. The operator then witnesses that profile’s Always-on VPN and
Block connections without VPN settings before further network checks.

Saved settings do not prove current enforcement. A helper checks only its own
recorded traffic and endpoints; it does not prove every app follows the same route.
Use the actual-app and failure-behavior checks recorded in your packet. Ask before
changing VPN providers, routing, lockdown, or permissions.

If networking fails, check the active profile, expected app, and available network.
Record the visible error without secret values. Do not disable a required VPN
protection simply to make connectivity appear successful; follow
[daily-use troubleshooting](daily-use.md#when-something-stops-working).

## Keep maintenance controlled

After handoff or a maintenance visit, physically confirm USB and Wireless debugging
are both off. Supervised maintenance may temporarily enable debugging; agree the
scope and have the final state checked again.

Arrange the update process with the operator for the OS and important apps. Reviewed
policies pin app versions, signers, and some configuration screens; changing them
can require new review and acceptance. Do not assume that a previous report covers
an updated build. Report unexpected changes and follow the agreed maintenance path.

## Share only what support needs

Send the asset label, affected profile/app, time, and a description of the problem.
Crop or omit private account information and credential fields from screenshots.
Use the agreed private process for any authorized registry update. Registry values
and populated inserts need separate protection from ordinary support material.

Read [backups, loss, and support](backup-loss-and-support.md) before repair, transfer,
reset, or reinstalling the OS. These actions can invalidate the previous identity,
credential record, and handoff evidence.
