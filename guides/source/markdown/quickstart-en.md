## 2 Choose the right profile

Android profiles separate app data and accounts. Use the names and purposes in your handoff packet; only the profiles actually supplied apply to your phone.

1. Unlock the current profile. Swipe down twice for Quick Settings and use the user switcher. Its location can vary; follow the handoff demonstration.
2. Select the intended profile and enter **that profile's ordinary lock**. Check the profile name before opening an app or entering credentials.
3. Confirm the app shows the intended account. The same app icon in two profiles does not mean the same signed-in account.
4. To finish a sensitive secondary session, use **End session** where offered. Ask support if you cannot find it. Switching profiles alone does not end the previous session.

After restart, unlock Owner before starting secondary profiles. Know Owner's ordinary credential even if you usually use another profile.

**Keep activities in their assigned profiles.** Copying files, using the same accounts or sharing identifiers between profiles can reconnect activities you wanted to separate.

App versions are shared; app data and many settings are separate. An update can affect several profiles. Ask support before removing a profile, replacing a supplied app or changing required routing.

A deleted profile's local data is lost. Reusing its name does not restore it. Report a missing or locked-out profile before deleting or recreating anything.

## 5 Check your connection and VPN

Each profile has its own VPN configuration. Owner's VPN does not establish another profile's connection. Follow the VPN and routing in your handoff packet.

1. Join the agreed Wi-Fi network or confirm mobile data. Complete network sign-in only through the agreed setup process.
2. In the intended profile, open the supplied VPN app. Sign in or import its configuration privately and accept Android's VPN consent when expected.

A VPN does not make you anonymous or hide a signed-in account from its app. Apps, websites and the VPN service have their own data practices. Tests cover only their recorded scope.

## 6 Understand your privacy

pxv prepares phones locally; it is not remote device management. Handoff checks record observations at preparation, not continuous monitoring or guaranteed future security.

**Apps and networks matter.** Profiles do not remove data you give an app or account provider. Review permissions, account sharing and services. Hide sensitive notification contents on the locked screen where appropriate.

**Agree what support retains.** Authorized support may keep a local encrypted record of phone identity/numbers, profile locks, Session IDs, Proton VPN credentials and separately identified erasure secrets. Agree who can unlock it and how updates, history, backups and retention work.

The record contains last-confirmed information, not live readings. Private changes can leave it outdated. Earlier revisions may remain encrypted; changing a code does not delete old copies.

Ordinary handoff reports omit secrets. Explicit private retrieval creates a readable file needing protection and cleanup. Anyone obtaining your paper insert can read it. Store it securely apart from the phone after handoff.

**Share only what support needs:** asset label, profile, affected app, time and the visible problem. Remove secrets and private message contents from screenshots. Use the agreed private process for credential updates, never a public issue or an ordinary support note.

Record real support details, retention and the private update method in your insert. Example contacts are not your service arrangement.

## 7 Keep the phone secure

**Keep software current.** Leave automatic OS updates enabled, restart when prompted and use agreed trusted app sources. Follow the maintenance plan; report failed updates promptly. Keep ordinary credentials available for restart.

**Protect access.** Lock unattended phones. Keep debugging off after setup or maintenance; reject unexpected debugging prompts. Contact support before bootloader changes, reset, repair, OS reinstallation or transfer.

**Review permissions.** Give camera, microphone, location, contacts and file access only as needed. If an app fails after a change, report it before granting broader permissions.

**Understand erasure.** Optional GrapheneOS duress credentials irreversibly erase the **whole device, all profiles and installed eSIMs** at applicable authentication prompts. They are not alternate unlock codes. Keep them separate and never try one to see what happens.

An optional **Oblivion notification-wipe key** is a different secret for a particular app installation and profile. The requested whole-phone wipe behavior is not qualified by pxv for an exact app/build. Do not rely on it as a guaranteed remote-wipe service and never send a real key during setup, demonstrations or support.

Only configure optional erasure through the agreed supervised process after ordinary locks and language are complete. Neither the guide nor the support registry activates erasure. If a screen or credential type is unclear, stop before entering anything.

## 8 Recovery and support

**Back up each profile.** Agree a supported backup/export method, coverage, destination, frequency and exclusions. Owner's backup does not automatically cover other profiles or apps. A success message is not a demonstrated restore.

Keep recovery material safely outside the phone. Ask for a representative restore check on an authorized separate test device when available. Do not erase this phone, delete a profile or unlock its bootloader to test recovery.

**If the phone is lost** contact support from another trusted device. Review account sessions, passwords and recovery with service providers. The support registry cannot locate or recover the phone. Do not assume remote erasure is available.

**If a lock is forgotten** stop guessing unrelated codes and contact support. Recovery depends on the available credentials and backups. Understand data loss before approving destructive recovery.

Before accepting the device, confirm together:

- Phone, handoff report and private insert have matching asset labels.
- Profiles open with ordinary locks; apps and VPN work within recorded test scope.
- USB and Wireless debugging are off; configuration notes record remaining limitations.
- You have support details, a private update method and recovery access outside this phone.
- The credential insert is sealed for you; its optional erasure page is clearly separated. After receipt, store it securely away from the phone.
