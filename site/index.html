01 / THE APPROACH

Clear steps. Real boundaries.

From a single phone to a small fleet, pxv helps operators keep the process deliberate and inspectable.

01 — PREPARE

Start with a policy

Define profiles, apps and device settings. Review your own OS pins, app trust and configuration before use.

02 — VERIFY

Keep the evidence

Use reviewed plans, device observations and recovery checks to understand what was configured and what still needs attention.

03 — HAND OFF

Make it usable

Provide the recipient with clear instructions, an accepted handoff report and a separately protected credential insert.

Built for local operation. pxv is not a remote MDM and does not coordinate locks across workstations. OS installation is a separate destructive phase; physical confirmations and private app setup remain explicit.
